# Cybersecurity Trade Clearinghouse (National Ingestion & Registry Service)

The **National Cybersecurity Trade Board (NCTB) Clearinghouse** is the central verification, Merkle chain traversal, practitioner registry, and labor dispatch service for **The Cybersecurity Trade Project**.

---

## 1. Core Capabilities & Architecture

The Clearinghouse operationalizes Pillar IV (Professional Licensure & The Journeyman Standard), Pillar V (Personal Liability & The Right of Technical Refusal), Pillar VI (Craft Guilds, Labor Trusts & Collective Defense), and Pillar VII (The Insurance-Driven Market Catalyst):

* **Cryptographic Submission Bundle Ingestion:**
  * Ingests encrypted quarterly submission envelopes generated by the Universal Digital Logbook.
  * Traverses continuous SHA-256 Merkle hash chains from Genesis to Chain Head to verify zero historical mutations or tampering.
  * Validates Ed25519 digital signatures applied across supervisor attestations.
* **National Practitioner & Public Key Directory:**
  * Authoritative trust root for licensed Journeymen, Master Practitioners of Record (MoR), and Registered Apprentices.
  * Tracks accredited Prior Learning Assessment (PLA) hours (enforcing the 2,000-hour cap) and active specialty endorsements (`SE-APP`, `SE-CLD`, `SE-ICS`, `SE-MED`, `SE-DFIR`, `SE-AIML`).
* **Craft Guild Dispatch Hall & Out-of-Work Queue (Pillar VI):**
  * Maintains the objective talent referral queue disallowing recruiter placement fees.
  * Enforces **First-In, First-Out (FIFO) Chronological Seniority of Availability** so candidates searching for work longest are prioritized for requisitions without arbitrary bypass.
  * Implements **Modality-Aware Routing** supporting Remote Only, Hybrid, and On-Site SCIF / Classified roles.
* **Participating Employer Council Directory & Ratio Telemetry:**
  * Authoritative registry of sponsoring enterprises (PEC Directory) and their active Master of Record designations.
  * Continuously tracks on-shift supervisory ratios against the mandatory 2:1 operational standard.
* **Form FORM-001 Evidentiary Refusal Ledger (Pillar V):**
  * Public immutable ledger recording cryptographic hashes of Notices of Safety Non-Concurrence executed under Pillar V.
  * Binds executive override risk assumption records (Form FORM-002) to establish liability transfer.
* **Statutory Wage Step Elevation & 8,000-Hour Math (Pillar II & IV):**
  * Evaluates runtime hour accumulation across the 5 core operational domains:
    * Domain 1: Perimeter, Cloud & Network Defense (1,500 hrs)
    * Domain 2: Detection Engineering & Incident Triage / SOC (2,000 hrs)
    * Domain 3: Identity, Credential & Access Management / IAM (1,500 hrs)
    * Domain 4: Vulnerability & Attack Surface Management (1,500 hrs)
    * Domain 5: Defensive Governance, Risk & Audit / GRC (1,500 hrs)
  * Formally issues tamper-evident Wage Step Elevation Seals for Tiers 1 through 4 (50%, 60%, 70%, 80%, 100% RJPB).
* **Underwriter & Actuarial Telemetry Feed (Pillar VII):**
  * Automated zero-knowledge compliance scoring verifying 2:1 on-shift operational supervision ratios and active MoR coverage for CUAAC cyber insurance rate discounts (25% to 35%).

  [ BOOK 1: RESIDENT LOCAL PRACTITIONERS (Primary Queue) ]
  • Fully licensed Journeymen & Registered Apprentices residing in that geographic Local.

  • Right of First Referral: Candidate longest out-of-work matching requisition receives placement.

  • Credentialed practitioners registered in sibling Locals traveling into the District.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  ROUTING PROTOCOL:
  • On-Site Requisition: Evaluates Book 1 resident queue (+ clearance if classified/SCIF).

## 6. Command-Line Interface (`ctl-clearinghouse`)

### Ingest & Verify a Submission Bundle
```bash
uv run ctl-clearinghouse ingest path/to/submission_bundle.json
```

### Evaluate Operational Hours & Issue Statutory Wage Elevation Seal
```bash
uv run ctl-clearinghouse evaluate --hours 4200 --practitioner-id CTP-APP-2026-0884
```
